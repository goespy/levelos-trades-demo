# Three-minute portfolio walkthrough

All records referenced below are generated by `npm run demo:setup`.

## 1. Business overview

Start at `/` and introduce the four operating areas: pre-sale, execution, customer experience, and back office. Point out pipeline value, active jobs, collected revenue, and conversion rather than reading every number.

## 2. Lead to proposal

Open `/clients`, choose an active synthetic client, and follow the linked design and build. In the build detail, show how dimensions, materials, equipment, and options become an inspectable line-item estimate. Open the associated proposal and its share view.

## 3. Contract to execution

Open `/contracts` to show the sample contract state, then `/jobs` to show construction phases and progress. Use `/field` to demonstrate the mobile operating view.

## 4. Customer and back office

Run `npm run demo:tokens` locally and open one generated `/j/...` URL in a private window. Return to the operations view and show `/invoices` and `/accounting`. The `/pay/...` experience is explicitly a simulator and never asks for payment credentials.

## 5. Close

Explain that the workflow is production-derived while the interface, people, projects, pricing, integrations, and legal content have been redesigned or synthesized for public review.
